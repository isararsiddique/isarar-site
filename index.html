About

I'm 22. I finished a biotechnology degree in Lucknow in 2025, at a college with no research pipeline and no obvious route into any lab I wanted to work in. So I stopped applying and started sending people finished work instead — read their last three papers, find a gap or a dataset they'd published but not fully mined, and offer two weeks of analysis for free. That's how I ended up at Harvard, Stanford, ISRO, IISc, and three IITs.

It's also how Zero Dementia got its hospitals. I built the screening infrastructure first and brought it to SGPGI Lucknow and two medical colleges before asking anyone for patients. That project is now in clinical trial on two tracks — cognitive screening and audiometry.

The work I care about sits at an awkward intersection: models that are accurate enough to be useful and transparent enough that a clinician will actually trust the output. Most medical AI fails on the second half. I spend a lot of time on explainability for that reason, and increasingly on the sensing side — PCB design, circuit simulation, the parts that decide what your data even looks like before a model ever sees it.

I write here about what I'm building, what breaks, and what I get wrong. Start with the writing if you want the useful version.

Building

Zero Dementia

In trial ↗

Multimodal screening for early cognitive impairment that runs on a stock smartphone — voice, expression, motor and cognitive signals, risk score in under 15 minutes. In clinical trial at SGPGI Lucknow with two medical college partners. Built at Biotech Wallah.

SuppliAi

Convolity AI ↗

Supplement and drug interaction analysis against a person's own health data — labs, prescriptions, wearable time series — producing evidence-ranked recommendations rather than generic advice.

HealMed

Public dataset ↗

Medical data manager and evaluator on a multilingual clinical dataset for healthcare LLMs, annotating Indian, Malaysian and Igbo clinical notes alongside practising clinicians. With Prof. Irene Li's lab.

Open source

AfDesign-PeptideBinder

Jupyter ↗

An 8-mer peptide binder designed against 5-hydroxyisourate hydrolase (PDB 2H6U_A) using AlphaFold-based AfDesign. Structure optimisation, binding interface evaluation, mutation sampling, pLDDT analysis.

SysBio-DiseaseNetwork

Jupyter ↗

Comparative protein interaction networks across Alzheimer's, type 2 diabetes and NAFLD. Hub-bottleneck genes (APOE, INS, LEP) identified by centrality and clustering — shared pathways between neurodegeneration and metabolic disease.

Red mud soil monitoring

Python · ESP32 ↗

IoT sensing rig built at IIT Bhubaneswar tracking NPK, pH and moisture in highly alkaline red mud soil. My first hardware project, and the reason I came back to electronics years later.

Neuro-AI

TypeScript ↗

Front end for the neuro screening work — the interface clinicians actually see when running an assessment.
